# Dragon parameters

This file is generated by `node gym/gym.mjs`. Do not edit it by hand. The Gym rebuilds its dataset from the private trade database when that exists, then the numbers, charts, `gym/out/presets.json`, `gym/out/estimator.json` and this file change together.

The Gym replays real Nearly.trade coins through the Dragon's rules in `gym/dragon-rules.mjs`, which mirrors the `dragon` section of `docs/SPEC.md`. Data runs 2026-09-22 to 2026-10-18. It covers 252,084 swaps of 2,884 coins. 887 cross-pair swaps had no NEAR value and are dropped. A coin launched against RHEA, QTC or another Nearly coin is priced in its own pool, with the other token valued in NEAR. For NEARLY, whose NEAR price is known directly, that valuation is off by 1.5% at the median (measured).

## Market model

- Pool fee. Every DCL swap pays 1%. DCL keeps 20% of it (measured, `docs/research/NEARLY.md`).
- Creator share of the pool fee. 60% of the remaining 0.8%, paid in NEAR from buys: 0.48% of buy volume (measured on three launches). Nearly also sells the coin side of sells for NEAR. That part is not measured and is left out of the replay.
- Token tax. WYRM has 400 bps on buys and 400 bps on sells (operator decision). Nearly keeps 40% and the tithe gets 60%, so the tax adds 2.4% of all volume.
- Dragon income. Half of the tithe: 0.24% of buy volume plus 1.2% of all volume. Buys are 50.7% of top-coin volume (measured), so the Dragon gets about 1.32% of volume.
- Cost of a Dragon buy. 1% pool fee plus 4% tax, 5% in all. The Dragon's own buys are buy volume too, so their creator share comes back in the next payout (inferred from the tithe split).
- Payout cadence. Nearly pushes creator fees about once an hour at :12 (measured). The replay pays income in hourly batches at minute 12.

## Top coins

A top coin is judged by its peak market cap in NEAR and by trading that lasted. The peak is the highest hourly median price, over hours with at least 3 priced minutes, times the 1B supply, so one stray trade cannot make a coin top. Sustained means at least 5 days that each traded 1% of the tier's market cap.

| rising | 10,000 NEAR | $47,306 | 29 | 25 |
| top | 30,000 NEAR | $141,918 | 14 | 14 |

- The Gym tunes on the 25 **rising** coins, 0.87% of all coins. Every held-out number is also given for the 14 **top** and 6 **breakout** coins. Their pairs: 16 wrap.near, 2 token.rhealab.near, 5 nearly-993927, 1 ninu-4, 1 qtc.omft.near.
- The median top coin peaked on day 4 of its life (p90 day 15). Most of each coin's recorded life comes after its peak.

## What limits each number

- Few coins. 25 top coins, 6 at the highest tier. One coin moves a fold's median.
- Hindsight. Top coins are chosen by a peak they reached. A coin that will be top is not known in advance.
- Short window. 27 days, and most top coins launched in the first ten. The budget result rests on the weeks after their peaks. Unspent NEAR is marked at the last price.
- No market feedback in the tuning replay. The Dragon's buys never move later prices.
- Depth is estimated from price moves, not read from the pool. Live quotes agree on shape and sit close on level.
- Income. The 0.48% pool-fee share and hourly cadence are measured. The 40% platform share of the tax is taken from other launches. The sell-side pool fee is not counted in the replay.
- The replay coins traded under their own taxes, not WYRM's. A 4% tax may change how traders behave.

## Rules the Gym assumes where the SPEC is silent

- `Day` needs the opening reserve (`base_reserve`) to size the extras, or the contract must size them from `budget`.
- The Hunt sell is held to the same impact cap, against the sell quote for 1 NEAR worth of WYRM. The SPEC defines the check for buys only.
- The spike guard reads "1,500 bps above the reference" as `tokens_per_near * 11_500 < reference * 10_000`.
- Dip extras count only while their step is confirmed in the same observation. A recovered price stops dip buying, and the breath budget continues.
- The reference uses samples within 24h of the newest sample. After more than 24h of silence the first new sample stands alone, so no dip can confirm for 10 minutes.
- A slice under 0.01 NEAR is skipped, as the contract does.

## Measured gas (sandbox)

This section is hand-written in `docs/GAS.md` and appended by `gym/parameters.mjs`. The numbers come from the near-workspaces sandbox (near-sandbox 2.13.4, protocol 86), on the release wasm built by `contracts/build.sh`. Rerun them with `npm run test:wsl -- integration bind_sandbox unsold_sandbox council_scenarios tax_sandbox dcl_real` and read the `GAS` lines in each sandbox log.

Burnt gas is what the transaction consumed across all its receipts. The transaction limit is on gas attached, and contracts attach fixed gas to the calls they make, so the attached column is the one a caller must meet. The v1 column is the frozen v1 trunk, rerun on the same machine on Nov 6. A dash means v1 had no such call.

Each column was read at one point in the transaction's life:

- **At commit.** The whole-transaction column and the v1 column come from near-workspaces, which answers at `broadcast_tx_commit`. That answer leaves out the gas-refund receipts that run later, about 0.22 Tgas each.
- **At FINAL.** The same calls read with `wait_until: FINAL`, which counts every receipt. They were re-read on Nov 9 in near-sandbox 2.13.4 (protocol 86) through `keeper/deploy.mjs` and one-call scripts, for the rows a deploy and a tithe round reach. A dash means the row was not re-read.
- **The called method's own receipt.** One receipt, which does not change between the two points.

A row that fans out to many accounts gains the most at FINAL. One `distribute` to 100 vaults read 49.04 Tgas at commit and 71.59 Tgas at FINAL: 101 more refund receipts. Every row with a dash reads higher at FINAL by its late refunds, most of all those that fan out: `sweep`, the tithe round after retirement and the real-DCL poke chains. Attached gas covers every receipt, so the 300 Tgas `distribute` attaches still leaves 228 Tgas spare at FINAL.

### The poke chain against real DCL

`dcl_real` imports the code of `dclv2.ref-labs.near` from mainnet (version 2.4.2, code hash `3G34Nhjmir5JPYPtijaA9iCNnHDPR7h3wfC9H77woEGo`, 1,166,649 bytes) with fresh state. It seeds one pool, `wrap|coin|10000`, with a single position from point -80,000 to -58,000 around 1,000 WYRM per NEAR. Three runs burnt the same gas to the hundredth.

The real chain burns 3.5 times the mock chain. Eight quotes account for 86 Tgas of the 102 Tgas gap and the swap for 12.5. Each quote burns about 12 Tgas against the 30 Tgas the Dragon attaches, and the swap 14.4 Tgas against the 180 Tgas the Dragon attaches. These match the mainnet receipts in docs/research/RHEA-DCL.md, where quotes burn 4.9 to 12.5 Tgas and swaps 8.1 to 14.1 Tgas. The Dragon's own receipts burn the same as on the mock. So the 760 Tgas floor holds with six halvings, and needs no cut to five. Quote gas grows with the points a swap crosses, so a pool with many positions, or a slice far larger than this one (29.4 NEAR), costs more per quote. WYRMLING's first buy on mainnet is the check for that.

What the numbers say:

- `create_vaults` is the tightest call. Each new vault burns about 8.6 Tgas, so a batch of 25 burns about 254 Tgas, 254.33 at FINAL, under the 280 Tgas limit, with 45 Tgas of headroom at 300 Tgas attached.
- `buy_scale` burns 5 Tgas more than v1, which is the mint sweep: a vault release and its callback.
- A tithe round after retirement sends fewer transfers, so it costs a sixth of a full round.
- The mock chain burns 0.43 Tgas more than v1. The Dragon's own receipts burn slightly less than v1, and the rest comes from the larger v2 mocks.

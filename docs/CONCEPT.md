# WYRM concept

This document sets the look and voice that the Lair, the demo video and the launch posts share. It offers five directions, each built as a working homepage, and recommends one.

The pages were sketches, each with a brand sheet and desktop and phone screenshots, and they are not in this repository. Only the chosen direction gets built properly, in `lair/`.

## 1. What every direction keeps

These rules come from the research in section 2. They hold whichever direction wins.

- **No green.** BLACKDRAGON, Shitzu, meme.cooking and near.cooking all use NEAR green on black. A green WYRM would disappear into the ecosystem. Bought, paid and passed use gold.
- **An old dragon, not a mascot.** No chibi, no big eyes, no goggles, no rocket, no black dragon, and no Lunar New Year or zodiac imagery. BLACKDRAGON owns all of those. The WYRM dragon is violet, heavy and indifferent, a hoarder that sleeps on gold.
- **Obsidian is glass.** The top Scale tier is drawn as violet-black glass with a sheen, never flat black, so it never reads as a "black dragon scale".
- **Proof, not claims.** Every memecoin lists "renounced, LP burnt" as a bullet. WYRM shows the zero keys with a link that proves it, and lists the refused proposals as evidence.
- **No memecoin furniture.** No contract-address pill, no trust checklist, no ticker band, no exchange logo wall, no tokenomics pie, no "how to buy in 3 steps", no "frens" or "join the pack", no hacker terminal, no speed claims.
- **The facts are the same everywhere.** A trade pays 4% tax. The tithe splits it 50/50 between the 100 Scales and the Dragon. The Dragon buys once a day in small slices and keeps every coin. In the Gym's replay of 62 real Nearly coins, buying once a day got a median 1.81 times more WYRM per NEAR than buying instantly. No proposal can move NEAR out of the Dragon.
- **The opening line of the video is "This dragon has no keys."** Four of the five directions arrived at it on their own.

## 2. What the research found

The research agent captured 29 project sites in headless Chrome and recorded their fonts, colours and copy.

- **Everyone sells speed.** pump.fun says "Trade faster", Intear "faster, safer", Hyperliquid "Open 24/7". Nobody sells patience, and patience is how the Dragon actually works.
- **Nobody shows the missing key.** Every memecoin says "renounced". No site makes the absence of a key the picture.
- **Nobody treats the treasury as a character.** Olympus shows a dry action feed and Ethena a counter. The Dragon's five moods come from contract state, which no project has.
- **Nobody cites data.** Ethena footnotes its yield. No memecoin cites anything. WYRM has the Gym.
- **A serious old dragon is unclaimed.** Crypto dragons are cute (BLACKDRAGON), zodiac (YOTD) or game characters (Puff the Dragon). Loot shows that a serif and a monospace font can carry fantasy without cartoon art.
- **Two name collisions to watch.** BLACKDRAGON (`blackdragon.tkn.near`, `@dragonisnear`) honours NEAR's co-founder and is active. Nearly also lists a small coin called "Near Dragons". Public copy leads with WYRM, never with "the dragon of NEAR", and the ticker and art get checked against both before launch day.

## 3. The five directions

### 1. One Breath a Day

**The idea.** Patience is the brand. The page is quiet most of the day and centres on a 24-hour dial and a large countdown to the next Breath. The background shifts with the UTC hour, from dawn lilac through noon to rose dusk and midnight.

**The Dragon.** A hollow line lying along the dial from now to the Breath, so the time left is the Dragon's length.

**Voice.** Calm, plain, slightly smug about waiting. "Once a day the Dragon wakes, buys WYRM in small slices, and keeps every coin." "Next Breath at 14:37 UTC. The Dragon has 41.2 NEAR to spend and nowhere to be. Still 0 keys."

**Fonts.** Bodoni Moda for the clock, headlines and numbers. Schibsted Grotesk for text and data.

**Palette.** Seven hour grounds from midnight #1C1830 to noon #EEF0F4. Wyrm violet #4E3F7A, hoard gold #9A6F12, ember #C8481A.

**Motion.** Moves only as fast as the clock. No entrance animations.

**Video.** The video runs through one UTC day from dawn to midnight. The dial's "now" marker sweeps between scenes, and the Breath beat lands on the gold arc.

**Best at.** Owning patience, the one idea no memecoin has, and making the 1.81x number the reason to hold.

**Worst at.** Energy. For most of the day the page is still and can read as a calm fintech site.

### 2. The Vault With No Door

**The idea.** An engraved banknote and bank-vault look. The hero is a round vault door with no handle and a large engraved "0 keys" at its centre, with an intaglio wyrm coiled round the rim.

**Voice.** Deadpan security humour. "A treasury with no door, and a dragon sleeping on it." "Audited the WYRM vault today. Found 0 keys, 0 handles, 1 dragon. The dragon did not look up."
- Refused proposals are stamped REFUSED in red on an "attempted robberies, filed" log.
- The main button reads "Try to rob it".

**Fonts.** Bodoni Moda for headlines, numbers and serials. IBM Plex Sans Condensed for text and microtext.

**Palette.** Security paper #E8E5EC, intaglio violet #2B1D45, vault steel #1E1530, foil gold #A67C1F, numbering red #B3311F.

**Motion.** Almost none, because a vault doesn't move. The breath clock ticks, and in Hunting the wyrm circles the door.

**Video.** The camera pushes into the "0" until it fills the frame. Facts are stamped over real explorer pages, for example "0 KEYS" in red over the nearblocks keys tab. A serial in the corner names each beat.

**Best at.** One unforgettable image. You see "keyless" before you read a word, and refused proposals become trophies.

**Worst at.** Small sizes. The fine engraving turns to mush in an X thumbnail, and at a glance it can pass for a bond.

### 3. The Bestiary Ledger

**The idea.** The Dragon is an entry in a medieval bestiary. Every buy, mood and failed robbery is a line in its ledger, in black and red ink on warm vellum. The moods are illuminated miniatures, and the margins carry live notes: the next breath, the mood and the last ledger line.

**Voice.** Grave and short, never fake old English. "It has no keys. It eats once a day. It gives nothing back." "Scale 042 is written to your name. 0.21 NEAR went to the Dragon."

**Fonts.**
- UnifrakturMaguntia for the wordmark and one decorated initial.
- IM Fell English for headlines.
- Crimson Pro for body text.
- Fragment Mono for numbers.

**Palette.** Vellum #E9DBB8, iron-gall ink #2B1D14, rubric red #A8261B, gold leaf #B8862A, wyrm violet #55306E.

**Motion.** The page is a book. Smoke drifts while the Dragon sleeps and fire licks while it breathes.

**Video.** Cuts on a red rule drawn across the frame. Each contract fact is written as a new ledger line, with its transaction hash in the margin.

**Best at.** Making the contract's record the brand. It looks unlike anything in crypto, and every refused proposal reads as dated history.

**Worst at.** Traders. A light vellum book is slow next to dark trading screens. It also turns into a costume the moment the copy slips into "ye olde".

### 4. The Living Lair

**The idea.** The Dragon's mood repaints the whole site, so the brand is the weather of the contract. Each mood changes the ground, accent, light, and even the weight of the display type:
- Sleeping is cold navy.
- Stirring holds its breath in gold.
- Breathing is ember and bright.
- Gorged is warm and heavy.
- Hunting is sharp and cold.

Embers drift across the background, and their count, speed and colour come from the mood. This direction grows out of the current Lair.

**Voice.** Direct and present-tense, a live report. "WYRM is Stirring. The price fell below step 1 and the Dragon is counting samples, 3 of 5." "Refused. The daily budget can't go above 40%. This proposal asked for 90%."

**Fonts.**
- Grenze Gotisch, a variable font, for the wordmark and mood names. Its weight follows the mood, from 200 asleep to 900 gorged.
- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all reading text.
- Martian Mono for the split-flap tiles and amounts.

**Palette.** One set per mood. For example, Sleeping is #0E1322 / #93A0CC and Breathing is #2A0E0A / #FF7A33. These stay constant in every mood:
- the violet dragon, #7C62C4 to #2A1F45;
- hoard gold #F2B440;
- ember #EC7044 for refusals.

**Motion.** Motion is data. Colours change only when the contract's state changes. Everything crossfades in about a second, and the split-flap tiles flip in sequence.

**Video.** No cuts between mood beats. The whole frame recolours as the contract moves from Sleeping to Stirring, Breathing and Gorged, and the mood word changes weight in place. Facts flip onto split-flap tiles.

**Best at.** Teaching the mechanism by feel and giving the video and every post a new picture each day. It also has the most energy of the five and fits dark trading screens.

**Worst at.** Recognition. WYRM has no single fixed colour, and a still thumbnail shows only one fifth of the idea.

### 5. The Naturalist's Field Guide

**The idea.** The Dragon is a studied animal, written up in field notes and scientific plates:
- Plate I is an anatomical drawing whose labelled parts are real contract methods. The eye is `on_observe()` and the jaw `poke()`. The hoard notes the missing `withdraw()`: "The organ was never grown."
- A specimen tray pins all 100 Scales.
- A migration map shows where a fee goes.
- The Gym's results are drawn as Plate VI, "Feeding behaviour, observed across 62 coins", with footnotes.

**Voice.** Dry, scientific and deadpan. "The Dragon eats once a day and has never been seen to give anything back." "Refused. budget_bps must be 1000 to 4000, got 9000. The Dragon cannot be taught to eat faster."

**Fonts.** IM Fell English for text and headlines, IM Fell English SC for plate numbers, Courier Prime for typed labels and values.

**Palette.**
- Plate paper #E3E0D1 and iron-gall ink #2A2320.
- Wyrm violet #5B3F7A for the Dragon.
- Gold leaf #A9802A for bought, paid and passed.
- Vermilion #B8431D for plate numbers, fire and refusals.

**Motion.** Almost none. Fee routes crawl along dotted lines, and the clock updates every 30 seconds because the animal is slow.

**Video.** Each beat is a numbered plate. Explorer captures sit in the plate as figures, with a lettered leader line pointing at the fact.

**Best at.** Credibility with the joke intact. No keys, the Leash and the 1.81x all read as observed facts with footnotes, which no memecoin does.

**Worst at.** Energy. It asks to be read, and in a quick scroll it can look like a museum page.

## 4. Side by side

The scores are my judgment from the screenshots and the research, on a scale from low to high. They are not measurements.

| | One Breath | No Door | Bestiary | Living Lair | Field Guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stands apart from BLACKDRAGON and the NEAR field | high | high | high | medium | high |
| Energy for traders on launch day | low | medium | low | high | low |
| Recognisable in an X thumbnail | medium | low | medium | medium | medium |
| Explains the mechanism without reading | high | medium | medium | high | medium |
| Carries the demo video | medium | high | medium | high | medium |
| Effort to turn into the real Lair | high | medium | high | low | high |
| Risk of looking like a costume or a bank | low | medium | high | low | medium |

## 5. Recommendation

Build the Lair as **the Living Lair**, with **the Vault's handleless door as the emblem**, and write the copy in **the Field Guide's dry voice**.

- **The Living Lair as the system.** It is the only direction with launch-day energy. It fits dark trading screens, and it teaches the mechanism without a word, because the page changes when the contract changes. It also costs the least, because the current Lair already has the violet dragon, the gold hoard and the moods. Its weakness is that it has no fixed mark to recognise across posts.
- **The door as the fixed mark.** The handleless vault door with "0" at its centre is the strongest single image in the five. It solves the Living Lair's recognition problem. It works as the logo, the X avatar, the video's opening shot and the "Verify" page hero, and it stays the same while the colours around it change with the mood. Its fine engraving needs a simplified version that still reads at avatar size.
- **The Field Guide's voice for the copy.** It is deadpan, and every claim carries its source. "The organ was never grown" and "The Dragon cannot be taught to eat faster" are the best lines in the five. They suit a coin whose whole argument is that the evidence is real.
- **What to keep from the other two.** One Breath's countdown to the next Breath belongs in the Sleeping mood. The Bestiary's ledger of dated lines, including refused proposals, is a good shape for the Lair's event log.

This is a recommendation, not a measurement. The choice is yours. If you prefer one direction pure, the Field Guide is the strongest alternative for judges and the Vault the strongest for a single viral image.

## 6. What happens next

1. You pick a direction, or a mix like the one above.
2. The chosen direction's type, palette and voice move into `lair/`. The Lair's demo data also moves to the current Dragon defaults (20% budget, steps at 20%, 35% and 50%) and to "price impact" wording for the slice cap. Some sketches still say "2.5% of the pool's depth", which is out of date.
3. The emblem is redrawn as a simple SVG that reads at 48 pixels.
4. The video script in the master plan, Part 3, gets the chosen opening card and transitions.
5. Before launch, the ticker, name and art are checked against BLACKDRAGON and "Near Dragons" on Nearly.

All sketch figures that are not from the Gym or the spec are placeholders. That covers slice counts, proposal numbers and the tier mix in the tray.
